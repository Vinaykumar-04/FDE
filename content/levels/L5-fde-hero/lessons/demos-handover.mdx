## Why it matters for an FDE

A demo should help a customer judge a result, and a handover should help them operate it afterwards.

## The idea

A good demo follows a realistic task and shows evidence of value. A handover covers operation, limits, owners, and support steps.

> A demo is a guided test drive; a handover is giving the driver the map, keys, and maintenance notes.

## Worked example

Show a user submitting one real example, reviewing the result, and correcting an exception. Then show where to find logs and who owns failed cases.

## Common mistakes

- Showing only a polished happy path or ending without documenting known gaps.
- Agree on the demo goal, use representative data, show one failure path, and leave concise operating notes.
