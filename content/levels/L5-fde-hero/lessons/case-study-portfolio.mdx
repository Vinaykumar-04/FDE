## Why it matters for an FDE

A portfolio gives interviewers concrete evidence that you can understand a problem and deliver a working result.

## The idea

A strong case study explains the user problem, constraints, your choices, the result, and what you would improve. Protect private customer details.

> It is like a field notebook: show the route, decisions, evidence, and lessons rather than only a photo of the finish line.

## Worked example

Describe a sample integration with a diagram, a short demo, test coverage, setup instructions, and a clear note about what is simulated.

## Common mistakes

- Claiming unverified impact or publishing customer data and credentials to make a project look impressive.
- Use synthetic data, explain your personal contribution, and link to a reproducible project with a readable README.
