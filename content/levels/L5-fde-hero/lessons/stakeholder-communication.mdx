## Why it matters for an FDE

A deployment can affect users, technical owners, security teams, and decision makers. Clear updates keep people aligned.

## The idea

Useful stakeholder communication adjusts detail to the audience while keeping impact, decision, owner, and next update clear.

> It is like a control tower: each person needs the right signal at the right time, not every radio conversation.

## Worked example

A launch is delayed by a data permission. Tell the sponsor the user impact and revised plan; give the technical owner the exact permission and test needed.

## Common mistakes

- Sending a long technical log to everyone or hiding a delay until the final meeting.
- State what changed, why it matters, who owns the next action, and when people will hear from you again.
