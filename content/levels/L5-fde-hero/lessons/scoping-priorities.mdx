## Why it matters for an FDE

Customer requests often exceed the time or information available. A clear scope gives both sides a realistic first outcome.

## The idea

Scope defines what will be delivered, what is excluded, assumptions, risks, owners, and a way to decide whether the result worked.

> It is like agreeing on a first trail section before promising to build the whole mountain road.

## Worked example

A customer asks for five dashboards by Friday. Agree on one decision the dashboard must support, the data needed, and a demoable first version.

## Common mistakes

- Promising every requested item or silently changing the deadline and quality bar.
- Write a small outcome, its acceptance check, dependencies, and explicit non-goals; review them with stakeholders.
