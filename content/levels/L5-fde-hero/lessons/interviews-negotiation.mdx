## Why it matters for an FDE

FDE interviews test both technical judgment and how you work with people. Preparation helps you explain your reasoning clearly.

## The idea

Use specific examples to show discovery, implementation, trade-offs, communication, and outcomes. Negotiation is a respectful conversation about the role and offer terms.

> An interview is a working session, not a memory contest; negotiation is aligning expectations before starting a partnership.

## Worked example

For an ambiguous customer scenario, ask clarifying questions, state assumptions, sketch a safe first step, and explain how you would validate it.

## Common mistakes

- Pretending to know an answer, giving only abstract claims, or accepting terms before understanding them.
- Practice concise stories, ask about responsibilities and support, and compare the written offer with your priorities.
