## Why it matters for an FDE

An FDE needs to understand the real workflow before deciding what to build.

## The idea

Discovery uncovers users, goals, current steps, pain points, data, constraints, and how success will be measured.

> It is like mapping a route with the person who takes it every day before you design a shortcut.

## Worked example

A team asks for automated invoice review. Ask who reviews invoices, what exceptions matter, which systems are involved, and what result would save time safely.

## Common mistakes

- Taking the requested feature as the problem statement without watching the current workflow.
- Ask open questions, request a recent example, and summarize what you heard for the customer to correct.
