## Why it matters for an FDE

A model may not know a customer’s private or recent information. Retrieval can provide approved source material for an answer.

## The idea

RAG retrieves relevant documents and supplies their passages as context for a model to answer from.

> It is like an open-book exam: the answer should be grounded in the pages provided, and the reader should show which pages were used.

## Worked example

Search a permitted policy collection, pass the best matching passages to the model, and display links to those passages with the answer.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming retrieval guarantees a correct answer or failing to enforce document access before search.
- Check access before retrieval, evaluate source relevance, cite evidence, and let the system say when evidence is missing.
