## Why it matters for an FDE

Search over customer documents often needs to find meaning, not just exact word matches.

## The idea

An embedding maps text into a list of numbers that represents aspects of its meaning. Similar vectors can indicate related content.

> Imagine each paragraph placed on a map: related ideas land near one another, even when their exact words differ.

## Worked example

A user asks “How do I reset access?” A semantic search can find a document titled “Account recovery” even though the wording differs.

## Common mistakes

- Treating similarity as proof that a document is correct, current, or allowed for this user.
- Filter by permissions and metadata, inspect retrieved passages, and measure search quality using real questions.
