## Why it matters for an FDE

FDEs work with customer data and systems. Safe defaults protect the customer and the team delivering the solution.

## The idea

Security reduces risk through identity checks, least privilege, encryption, safe defaults, and a clear record of important actions.

> It is like a building with a visitor desk, locked rooms, and a sign-in record rather than one key for everyone.

## Worked example

A support tool needs to read ticket summaries. Grant its service identity read-only access to that workspace and log access decisions.

## Common mistakes

- Giving broad access because it is faster during a demo and forgetting to remove it later.
- Map what data is used, who needs access, and how access is granted, reviewed, and revoked.
