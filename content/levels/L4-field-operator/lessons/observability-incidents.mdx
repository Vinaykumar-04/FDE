## Why it matters for an FDE

When a customer reports a problem, you need evidence about what the system did and how users were affected.

## The idea

Logs record events, metrics summarize measurements over time, and traces connect work across services. Incident response coordinates impact reduction and recovery.

> Logs are individual notes, metrics are a dashboard of vital signs, and traces follow one request through the system.

## Worked example

A customer sees slow syncs. Compare request duration metrics, inspect a trace for the slow dependency, and update the incident timeline.

## Common mistakes

- Changing several systems at once before preserving evidence or communicating customer impact.
- Confirm impact, assign an owner, use observable evidence, communicate updates, and record follow-up actions.
