## Why it matters for an FDE

Some customers use Kubernetes to run and manage containerized services across a cluster.

## The idea

A cluster runs workloads described as desired state. Kubernetes controllers work to keep the actual state close to that description.

> It is like a venue manager keeping the requested number of staffed rooms available as people arrive and leave.

## Worked example

A deployment asks for two copies of an API. A service gives those copies a stable network address while Kubernetes replaces an unhealthy copy.

## Common mistakes

- Changing a live pod by hand and assuming the change will survive a replacement.
- Understand the deployment, service, configuration, and health checks before investigating a workload.
