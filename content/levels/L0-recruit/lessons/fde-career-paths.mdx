## Why it matters for an FDE
Knowing nearby roles helps you choose the right jobs and explain your strengths. Companies use titles differently, so compare the work each role describes.

## The idea
Imagine a relay team. A Solutions Engineer may help explore whether a product fits. An Implementation Engineer may configure it. An FDE often combines customer discovery, coding, deployment, and follow-through. A Software Engineer may focus more on building the product used by many customers.

## Worked example
A job called “Deployment Engineer” asks you to write Python integrations, meet customer teams, and own launches. Those duties overlap with FDE work. A different job with the same title may focus mainly on release tooling. Read the responsibilities before deciding.

## Common mistakes
- Assuming every company defines FDE the same way.
- Applying based only on the title.
- Describing yourself as “just a coder” when the role also needs communication.
