## Why it matters for an FDE
An FDE, or Forward Deployed Engineer, works close to a customer. You learn how their work happens, then help turn a real problem into a working software solution. The work includes code, but also questions, testing, and clear communication.

## The idea
Think of an FDE like a bridge builder. One side is the customer’s daily work. The other side is the product and engineering team. The FDE understands both sides and helps them meet safely in the middle.

## Worked example
A delivery company tracks late packages in spreadsheets. You first ask who updates the sheet, where the data comes from, and what “late” means. You then build a small integration that brings shipment updates into their system, test it with real examples, and show the team how to use it.

## Common mistakes
- Starting to code before understanding the customer’s workflow.
- Promising a feature before checking technical limits.
- Leaving without explaining how to operate the solution.
