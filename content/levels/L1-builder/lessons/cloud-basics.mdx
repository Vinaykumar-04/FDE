## Why it matters for an FDE

Customer systems may run on cloud services with different networks, permissions, and costs. FDEs need to ask how those pieces fit together.

## The idea

Cloud platforms provide computing, storage, networking, and managed services on demand. An account or project groups resources and access policies.

> A cloud account is like a campus: projects are buildings, services are rooms, and permissions decide who can enter.

## Worked example

A customer wants an API deployed privately. Identify the cloud account, network boundary, service identity, data store, and people who approve access.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing a service before understanding region, data rules, networking, and operational ownership.
- Draw a simple deployment diagram and confirm the customer’s access, location, and cost constraints.
